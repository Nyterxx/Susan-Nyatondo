Susan Nyatondo Incorporated is a distinguished Namibian law firm providing comprehensive legal services to individuals, businesses, and organisations across Namibia. Founded on the principles of integrity, excellence, and client-centred advocacy, the firm is authorised and regulated by the Law Society of Namibia.

Practice Areas
Civil Litigation
Strategic representation in High Court and Magistrate proceedings, enforcement of rights, and alternative dispute resolution.
Corporate & Commercial Law
Company incorporation, governance structuring, shareholder agreements, and regulatory compliance.
Criminal Law
Robust defence representation in criminal proceedings, bail applications, and advocacy at all court levels.
Wills & Estates
Drafting of wills, deceased estate administration, and probate proceedings to protect your family's legacy.
Contracts & Agreements
Drafting, reviewing, and negotiating commercial contracts, service agreements, and joint venture arrangements.
Compliance & Risk
Regulatory compliance advisory, risk assessments, and governance frameworks for businesses operating in Namibia.
Family Law
Sensitive guidance on divorce, child custody, maintenance, and domestic partnership matters with discretion and care.
Labour Law
Employment contract advice, disciplinary hearings, unfair dismissal claims, and labour court representation.
